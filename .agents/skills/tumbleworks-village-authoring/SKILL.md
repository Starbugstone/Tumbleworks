# Tumbleworks village authoring

## Shipping film target

The player explicitly chose authored, mechanically plausible animation for village
films. Production uses `VillageCinematic`; `VillageProgram` and its force/torque,
load, hinge and native mechanical certificates are offline research. They do not
gate a film, boot, or cinematic acceptance. Never resume the old certification
effort as a prerequisite for delivering films.

Keep actual collision-free meshes, continuous marble motion on the rendered
surfaces, deterministic seeking, readable causes, calm cameras, phone touch and
the fixed rendering/download budgets. Puzzle physics and saved progress stay
unchanged. Cinematic and physical modes are explicit; neither silently falls
back to the other. Use the `cinematic` test suite for shipping films.

Create a village the player recognises by its shape and machinery, whose marble
rolls continuously between real interactions. Finish a playable sequence through
the running app. A compiled plan, attractive still or passing fixture alone does
not finish a village.

Read [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) and
[the development workflow](../../../docs/development-workflow.md). They own product
rules and authorization. This skill adds no deployment, campaign registration,
coefficient changes or permission checkpoints.

## Start from the live tree

Establish the chapter, theme, affected files and player-visible acceptance check.
Read current types/tests; old design notes and receipts can describe superseded
implementations. Check for an active source/browser/CPU owner, coordinate competing
work and preserve the owner's dev server and snapshot preview. Routine work
already authorized by the task does not need fresh permission.

```sh
python3 .agents/skills/tumbleworks-village-authoring/scripts/village_check.py status
python3 .agents/skills/tumbleworks-village-authoring/scripts/village_check.py status --chapter 1 --require-physical
```

The second command explicitly selects physical research and fails if the chapter
cannot compile physically. An `authoring` tail in that mode is an unresolved
research compiler failure, not a cinematic playback gate. These reports prove neither clearance,
touch, comfort nor performance. Diagnose the first exact failure before changing
mechanisms downstream.

## Settle one coherent village

Resolve proposed UX/layout with Claude Opus 5.5 at high effort, as AGENTS.md
requires. Reuse applicable settled decisions; do not repeat a review for each
routine fix. If that model is unavailable, report it and continue independent
work without inventing agreement or substituting another model.

Describe the terrain silhouette, route topology, track/support kit and signature
causal actions. Distinguish them from every existing village in grayscale and at
overview scale. A rotated route or recolour is insufficient. Preserve interesting
spins, speed changes and building actions, driven by visible causes.

Read [architecture and authoring contracts](references/architecture.md) when
writing data, physics, scenery or assets. Keep one owner integrating the complete
village. Delegate a bounded specialty only when useful and authorized; repeated
partial handoffs do not deliver a playable film.

## Build and prove in increasing cost

Use [the checks and tool map](references/checks.md). Retain a regression that
rejects the old defect and accepts the real repair. Distinguish:

- a rolling pass with continuous incoming/outgoing velocity;
- a genuine catch with finite braking, spin settling and visible release;
- a powered carry with a visible, mechanically plausible source and shared mesh/actor frames.

Reuse supported primitives where they fit the visible design. Physical research
still requires its complete contact/drive model; film animation does not.

The same contact frames drive motion and mesh. Cinematic timing is authored from
path length and explicit catch/carry/release clips. Never teleport or hide the
marble, change puzzle coefficients, or call animation a successful physical solve.

Check cheap rest geometry, feet and counts before expensive whole-motion sweeps.
Then include all active linkage, terrain, scenery and earned rewards. A clear
centre does not prove full-sphere or track-width clearance. Prove connected joints
and intentional contacts explicitly; names/groups are not blanket exemptions.
Separate model, Float32 packing, probe and product defects before changing a
tolerance.

## Finish in the player's view

Follow smoothly with at most two soft zoom-in excursions. Do not refit distance
at each stop or move the camera to conceal geometry defects. Preserve still-camera
reduced motion, exact map endpoints and the compiled timeline. Use the film-camera
skill if available; portable rules and current checks are in AGENTS.md and
[the tool map](references/checks.md).

Freeze served code/config during browser journeys. Use
[verify-tumbleworks](../../../.claude/skills/verify-tumbleworks/SKILL.md) for launch,
doctor, native-touch input and cleanup. Inspect the complete current film at
375×690 in both modes and on the existing desktop layout. Recheck map targets,
stems, Workshop entry and yaw after their geometry or markers change.
Presentation saves do not prove unlock boundaries or real puzzle wins.

Run affected checks/budgets, then final shared gates when combined source is
stable. Profile frame/tick changes against a recorded baseline; browser timings
are not physical-phone performance. Repeat a passed check when a new change,
failure or coverage gap warrants it, rather than rerunning everything each edit.

Report behavior, exact source/evidence, remaining failures and checks actually
run. Keep authored, physics compiled, geometry verified, browser verified and
performance verified separate. Preserve failures/timeouts/history. One village's
success never proves the others.
